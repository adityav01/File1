eSSL connection

Uses the eTimeTrackLite SOAP method GetTransactionsLog. Typical URL: http://SERVER:PORT/iclock/WebAPIService.asmx or eBioServer /WebAPIService.asmx.

Device notes

  • The biometric machine must already be enrolled in eSSL software (eTimeTrackLite / eBioServer).
  • This app polls punch logs; it does not flash firmware on the terminal.
  • If the device can push JSON, POST to /api/essl/push with UserId, LogTime, and Type IN/OUT.
